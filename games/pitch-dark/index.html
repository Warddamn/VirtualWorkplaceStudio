A murder mystery for your ears

Pitch Dark

The lights are out, and someone in the room is a killer. You can't see a thing. Listen, ask questions, and feel your way to the truth.

  • Put on headphones. Dim the lights, or just close your eyes.
  • Hold Space, or the glowing button, and talk to anyone in the room. Let go when you're done.
  • Name the killer before the deadline. You get about fifteen questions. Then the doors open and the killer walks free.
