# Assumptions: p4-provenance-recert (DD1 Provenance & Recertified Memory), 2026-11-01

Every number below is also in the INPUTS block of `models/provenance-recert.py`, where each value carries its source.

## Evidence limits
- [p4-provenance-recert] I used all 30 WebSearch calls. My single WebFetch (govinfo.gov) failed DNS, and per the tool's instruction I stopped trying more hosts. Every external fact here is therefore a search snippet, and nothing is [VERIFIED] — basis: tool limits.

## Time and scenarios
- [p4-provenance-recert] Month 1 is Nov-2026 and month 36 is Oct-2029. §5949 takes effect in month 14 (Dec-2027), and the DRAM turn starts in month 15 (Jan-2028) — basis: 01_LANDSCAPE §0 item 4 and §7.2.
- [p4-provenance-recert] In BASE, used-module prices stay flat at their trailing-12-month midpoints through Dec-2027. They fall 10% across 2028, then another 10% by Oct-2029 — basis: BofA −10% 2028 ASP (01_LANDSCAPE §1 row 4b). I hold prices flat rather than extending the 2026 rise, which is conservative.
- [p4-provenance-recert] In GLUT, used-module resale falls to 0.40× of today over the 12 months of 2028, using the ratio of glut to contract price from `ddr5_reference()`. In SEVERE it falls to 0.10×. Both scenarios floor used resale at $1.0/GB, the 2023-like trough from C_notes — basis: A-05, the 2018–19 analog of −62.5% in about 4 quarters, and the canonical $2.04/GB severe deck.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Used-module prices follow the DDR5 contract index one-for-one. Used DDR4 rose 285–335% YoY into Aug-2026 (amcompute), so a 60% fall would only take it back to about late-2025 levels — basis: amcompute appraisals.

## Recert (C-62) and PMem (C-26)
- [p4-provenance-recert] Resale midpoints are $125 for a 32GB DDR4 RDIMM, $310 for 64GB DDR4 and $1,045 for 64GB DDR5. The unit mix is 60/30/10 — basis: amcompute realized ranges for the 12 months to Aug-2026; DDR5 decommission supply is thin.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Lots are bought at **65%** of realized resale. 01_LANDSCAPE's 60% was flagged as optimistic: Iron Mountain ALM is capturing the repricing, and lot listings run near resale (PCSP: 24×32GB at ~$140 each). **This is the single most important unverified input in the recert line.** The kill experiment exists to measure it.
- [p4-provenance-recert] B2B buyers (TPMs, refurbishers) pay 0.88× the eBay realized price, with 3% payment fees. The eBay fee is 13.6% plus $0.40 per order. I used the higher of two conflicting fee sources — basis: LitCommerce calculator (13.6%) vs EcomCalcTools (10.9%).
- [p4-provenance-recert] Labor is 12 minutes per module, covering receiving, SPD read and log, loading and unloading burn-in, labels, photos and listing, and packing. Founder ops time is 35 h/month part-time and 60 h/month full-time. Hours beyond that go to a technician at $25/h loaded — basis: C_notes used 6 minutes for testing alone; I doubled it for handling.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Test fallout is 3% of purchases and returns are 2% of revenue — basis: C_notes Appendix A. Warranties in the channel run from 90 days to lifetime (Reboot IT, Rimlance), so I assume a 1-year warranty.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Inventory is bought in month t and sold in t+1. Purchases are capped by cash minus a $5k reserve. The operator buys only 10% of target when expected contribution falls below 5%, judged on the trailing price trend rather than a forecast — basis: rational wind-down. Inventory held through a falling month still takes the loss.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Volume ramps by 40 modules a month, capped at 400 per month part-time and 800 full-time — basis: channel absorption across eBay plus 3–5 B2B accounts; not tested.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Optane PMem 128GB resells at $200 and is bought at 55% of that, 25 units a month, all through eBay. Volume drops to 40% after vSphere 8 end of general support (2027-10-11), and price follows the DDR4 index — basis: listings show $290 new on eBay.de (undated) and $65–380 used (C-26). Broadcom KB 459135.

## CleanBOM-5949 (C-64)
- [p4-provenance-recert] Launch is month 10 (Aug-2027). §805's indirect ban starts 2027-06-30 and §5949 on 2027-12-23, but buyers are waiting on the final FAR rule — basis: C-64 verify correction; Crowell on §805.
- [p4-provenance-recert] There are two tiers: $750/month for integrators and resellers and $2,500/month for module makers, OEMs and ODMs, with 35% of customers on the high tier. That blends to $1,362/month. One-off evidence packs sell at $900, bought by 15% of conversations — basis: priced below SiliconExpert-class ACVs (Accuris offer floor of $6k/yr) and far below Exiger's ($12.7M Army) and Interos's ($12M DLA/3 yr) enterprise deals. The C-64 card's $1.5–12k/month tiers were cut for the safe harbor.
- [p4-provenance-recert] The founder holds 5 qualified conversations a month part-time and 12 full-time. Close rate is 10% in the bundle and 7% standalone, where die-source evidence is thinner; that 3-point lift *is* the bundle hypothesis and is not measured. Churn is 2% a month — basis: B2B SMB SaaS norms; untested.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Die-ID lab work (X-ray, decap and die-marking photo) costs $400 a sample and is resold at 1.6×. Each subscriber orders 0.4 samples a month. The library builds at 3 samples a month from month 4 to 18, then 1 a month. Standalone CleanBOM also buys each sample module ($150) — basis: weak blog ranges of $50–500 per component and $150–500 per SEM sample; ANAB lists 26 AS6171 labs; no lab quote was obtained.
- [p4-provenance-recert] CleanBOM demand is the same in all three price scenarios, because it is set by statute, not by DRAM prices. A glut raises CXMT share, which arguably *raises* demand. I do not credit that.

## DIMM-health (C-85)
- [p4-provenance-recert] Launch is month 12. Price is $0.60 per DIMM-month, below the card's $0.75, because Lenovo MPFA, Intel MFP (in AMI and Insyde BMC firmware) and Park Place ParkView already provide prediction. A customer has 1,200 DIMMs. Sales add 0.15 new customers a month part-time and 0.35 full-time. Churn is 1.5% a month, rising to 3.5% after Jan-2028 in GLUT and SEVERE as refresh gets cheaper — basis: C-85 verify (DIY sparing is ~3.6× cheaper); Lenovo Press LP2326; Intel MFP case studies.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Replacements run at 1.5% per DIMM-year. The spare pool is 2% of covered DIMMs, carrying cost is 20% a year, and shipping is $18 a swap. In the bundle, spares cost the recert buy price; standalone, they cost the eBay resale price — basis: C-85 card; Google 2009 (~8% of DIMMs see errors each year).

## Opex, people, certifications
- [p4-provenance-recert] The founder goes full-time on a $10k/month salary ($120k loaded, A-03) once trailing-3-month EBITDA before salary is at least $12k and cash is at least $25k. They return to the day job, and salary goes to 0, if cash falls below $8k — basis: A-03.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Fixed opex is tools $150/month (+$350 once CleanBOM is live), insurance $200 (+$400 E&O for attestations), bookkeeping $150 and marketing $300. Flex space costs $1,500/month above 250 modules a month or once the founder is full-time. Legal is $2.5k at setup and $6k before CleanBOM launch. ISO 9001/AS6081 readiness is $10k in month 15, plus $3k for surveillance in month 27 — basis: Amtivo's AS9120 $6–12k as a proxy; NQA is the AS6081 certification body.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Capex is $4.2k in month 1 (two used Gen10 DDR4 servers without memory, plus ESD bench and label printer) and $4k in month 6 (a DDR5 platform). No RAMCHECK is bought: memtest86+ plus stressapptest on server beds is assumed enough. RAMCHECK LX DDR4 is about $2.5k if a buyer demands one.
- [p4-provenance-recert] **R2v3 is not required** to buy tested, working RDIMMs for reuse from R2 ITADs. The ITAD's downstream due diligence covers it. R2v3 would be needed only to take untested or scrap circuit-board lots, which are Focus Materials — basis: [UNVERIFIED-training] reading of R2v3; SERI license is $2k/facility/yr plus CB audit if ever needed.
- [p4-provenance-recert] Recovered DIMMs are never sold into DoD deliverables, and every module is labeled "used/recertified" — basis: DFARS 252.246-7007/-7008 and FAR 52.246-26 [UNVERIFIED-training]; 18 U.S.C. §2320 (C_notes §6).
- [p4-provenance-recert] Founder time is imputed at $75/h, used only for CAC, never as cash. A CleanBOM sales conversation takes 3 hours — basis: estimate.
