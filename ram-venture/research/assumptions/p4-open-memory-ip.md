# Assumptions: p4-open-memory-ip (DD5 U.S. Open Memory IP), 2026-11-01

- [p4-open-memory-ip] Month 1 of the model is Dec-2026; the glut starts at month 14 (Jan-2028) per A-05 — basis: study date 2026-11-01; A-05 "from 2028-Q1".
- [p4-open-memory-ip] DRAM scenarios move only the commercial segment, through design-start budgets: demand x(1 − 0.33·drop), price x(1 − 0.15·drop), churn x(1 + 0.5·drop); academic demand is unaffected — basis: macros are priced per design, not per GB (sky130 SRAM ~$1,330/GB is 73–653x DDR5 in every scenario); NSF/DoD budgets are not tied to DRAM prices.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] BASE 2028 DDR5 drop = 10% — basis: 01_LANDSCAPE §1 row 4b (BofA −10% ASP 2028).
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Full-chip projects/yr on U.S.-fab open/academic shuttles ≈ 298 (wafer.space 2×54, ChipFoundry 60, Cadence 30, GF university pilot 40, MOSIS 2.0/Commons/other 60) — basis: wafer.space G802 repo (54 slots); Synopsys–GF pilot (40 universities); ChipFoundry, Cadence and other counts are estimates (no published counts found).
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Tiny Tapeout tiles (899 projects across SKY26a, SKY26b, IHP26a and GF0p2) are a funnel, not macro buyers — basis: a tile is far too small to host an 8KB+ macro, and TT designs pay about €70 per tile.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] 30% of full-chip projects need memory beyond the free macros, and 40% of those pay — basis: ~10 of 51 wafer.space Run 2 listings mention SRAM/RAM; the willingness-to-pay share is a judgment and the kill experiment tests it.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Active-account cap = paying projects/yr × 2.0 years (≈72: 43 academic, 29 commercial) — basis: labs and startups tape out repeatedly across a grant or funding cycle.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Prices: academic subscription $200/mo, commercial $800/mo; tapeout service $3k academic, $10k commercial; characterized macro license $20k — basis: these are pegged to MPW slot prices ($7–15k) and sit far below commercial compilers. Foundry-sponsored compilers are free to NDA-PDK customers, so open-PDK teams are the only market.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Contract VLSI rate $125/h; service jobs take 12 h (academic) or 30 h (commercial); licenses take 30 h plus a 10% warranty reserve — basis: U.S. freelance memory/analog layout rates [ESTIMATE].
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Acquisition: 30 contacts/mo part-time (60 full-time) × 25% reply × 15% close, plus inbound ramping to 1.2 accounts/mo from month 4 — basis: named, public tapeout teams (wafer.space/TT repo owners, GF pilot universities). The close rate is unverified.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Churn: 3%/mo academic, 2.5%/mo commercial — basis: student turnover and startup mortality [ESTIMATE].
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Characterization timeline: GF180 half slot at m4, characterized by m10; ChipFoundry sky130 slot at m7, characterized by m13 — basis: wafer.space Run 1 spent 12 weeks at GF; the Cadence/SkyWater MPW ran from a January submission to July die.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] The founder goes full-time when trailing-3-month gross profit ≥ $11.85k/mo (salary $10k plus full-time opex) and cash ≥ $25k — basis: A-03.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Free-bundle shock (the biggest risk) is modeled from m18: close rate ×0.5, churn ×2, subscription price ×0.8, licenses ×0.5 — basis: the C-33 fatal flaw. OCD (Apache-2.0 GF180 SRAM, last commit 2026-10-30), Chips4Makers (Arrakeen generator, wafer.space G802) and UCSC OpenRAM (116 commits in 12 months) are all actively closing the gap for free.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Gain-cell density advantage at mature nodes is 1.25–1.43x the 6T bitcell (65nm silicon, Teman group), with vendor claims of 1.67–2.0x (RAAAM/GF), not the 2–4x in the DD5 hypothesis. At sky130 a logic-rule gain cell may be near parity with the 1.896 µm² pushed-rule SRAM cell [ESTIMATE, untested]. Chips4Makers' "regular vs SRAM design rules" test chips on G802 show the same rule gap.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] Larger, banked SRAM macros (32–64KB at ~60% array efficiency ≈ 316 kbit/mm²) give ~5.5x the density of today's 2KB OpenRAM macro (57.6 kbit/mm²) with no new cell. That is the near-term density lever and needs software, not physics — basis: computed from VERIFIED LEF sizes.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] STTR Phase I $305k (NSF) awarded at m15 if submitted m6–m9 with a university PI; only the ~7% fee is free cash; ≥30% goes to the university — basis: NSF 26-510 summary; STTR split rule not re-verified this session.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] T2 overlay: one gain-cell evaluation license ($75k) at m34; seed $2.5M at m28, conditional on ≥$250k ARR and gain-cell silicon; circuit cofounder $220k/yr loaded; Weebit fee anchored at the ~$100k first-ever fee — basis: estimates; Weebit's fee for a fabless startup is unknown.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] The Cadence SKY130 MPW ReRAM-enabled option ($10–12k per block) is treated as a live cheap ReRAM prototype path, but a run after Jul-2026 is unconfirmed — basis: SkyWater/Cadence page via snippet. This corrects 01_LANDSCAPE §10.3 rule 8 ("no T0 path to a ReRAM test chip") for 2026. Whether die from that path may be used commercially without a Weebit licence is unknown.
- [p4-open-memory-ip] SkyWater's DMEA Category 1A Trusted status is assumed to survive the IonQ ownership — basis: IonQ/SkyWater statements only; no post-close DMEA confirmation was found.
