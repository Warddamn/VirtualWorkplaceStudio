## 4. Charge-transfer efficiency -> BER -> minimum CTE

Model: per-transfer deferral eps = k_ws x (1 - CTE); after n_t transfers a '1' keeps h0 = (1-eps)^n_t and the rest trails into later packets. The noise is read noise plus fat-zero noise, plus binomial partition noise N_s h0 (1-h0) and Poisson noise on the deferred charge. Partition noise is physics that no correction removes. Without DFE the threshold is fixed: the worst '1' follows zeros and the worst '0' follows ones, so the eye is (2 h0 - 1) N_s. With DFE the threshold follows the decided history, and the eye is h0 N_s - 2 kappa x tail (LSST-style deferred-charge correction, [Snyder 2021](https://spiedigitallibrary.org/journals/Journal-of-Astronomical-Telescopes-Instruments-and-Systems/volume-7/issue-04/048002/Characterization-and-correction-of-serial-deferred-charge-in-LSST-camera/10.1117/1.JATIS.7.4.048002.full)).

## 4c. RED-TEAM: regeneration topology (RT-CIRC-charge-river-02, RT-PROC-charge-river-03)

## 5. Trap kinetics: carrying imec's 6.25 MHz point to a 50 MHz stage clock

Transport is not the limit. Drift across a 25 nm gate at 10 cm^2/Vs and 1 V takes ~6.2e-13 s, and diffusion takes ~2.4e-11 s, both far below the 6.7 ns dwell at 50 MHz. Loss comes from traps that capture electrons while the packet sits and emit them after it has moved two gates on. With SRH emission tau_e = exp(E/kT) / nu0, nu0 = sigma v_th N_c = 1.1e+11 /s (v_th 2.1e+07 cm/s), the gates that lose charge are those with tau_e near the dwell time, i.e. trap depth E_w = kT ln(nu0 t_dwell).

## 12. GO / NO-GO

**GO/NO-GO:** GO if planar test CCDs (sheet charge ~4.5e+12 cm^-2, i.e. <= 447 e- per 40 nm-plate packet; worst-case 1-after-0s pattern) show a median per-transfer CTE >= **0.9973 with DFE** (deferral <= 2.7e-03 per transfer) at a >= 50 MHz STAGE clock (>= 150 M transfers/s) at BOTH 25 C and 85 C, and a 98-transfer loop with FPGA regeneration logs raw BER <= 5e-10. The chief's 0.999 spec is the STRONG-GO line: it buys 64-stage segments or 3x the worst-string spread. NO-GO if every channel variant stays below CTE 0.9951 at >= 50 M transfers/s, where density drops under 1.5x 1-beta and cost rises to ~$2.13/GB, worse than DDR5. System half: GO if trace replay shows >= 92% of bytes known >= one lap (0.64 us) ahead.

Headline: 1.14 Gb/mm^2 user (2.5x 1-beta), ~$1.74/GB (0.85x vs DDR5; >1 = cheaper), 313 e- packets (125 mV at the FD, 10 e- noise), ~0.09 pJ/bit core read, scrub power ~158x below DDR5 refresh, and a minimum CTE of 0.9973 (DFE) / 0.9981 (no DFE) at 98 transfers.

**RED-TEAM GO/NO-GO additions.** (1) GO line for the base (joint) topology stays at median CTE >= 0.9973 (DFE, Gaussian); with the RTN tail at eta 2.8 mV and a 25 e- screen it is 0.9975. Without the joint tier (one-way 64-stage fallback) the GO line is 0.9987, i.e. the old STRONG-GO region. (2) Sense-node RTN: measured 1e-9 per-read step quantile <= 30 e- after sparing <= 2% of >= 1e4 nodes, at the real 2-5 ns CDS interval. (3) Buried vs surface deferral >= 3x at matched 4.5e12 cm^-2 on the overlapping-gate reference, with an XPS-measured dEc >= 0.5 eV. (4) Density GO is >= 2.5x 1-beta; the cost claim is withdrawn (section 11).
