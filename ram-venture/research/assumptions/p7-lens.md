- [p7-lens] "Services" (lab, audit, per-tapeout support) is not one of the eight §3E lenses. Where a concept is mostly services, BEST went to the lens that names its paying buyer (defense-first for C-13 and C-92) or its recurring layer (software/SaaS for C-84) — basis: mission §3E names only eight lenses, and the task requires exactly one BEST per row.
- [p7-lens] When a concept's product type and its market both match a lens (e.g., C-67/C-55/C-37 hardware sold to defense buyers), BEST goes to the product lens (module/appliance) and the market lens (defense-first) is marked VIABLE — basis: one BEST per row; the product lens decides who pays for what.
- [p7-lens] N/A means the lens cannot apply without turning the concept into a different one. When a lens re-shapes a concept into one that was already killed, the verdict is WEAK and the kill is cited, except for pure-software concepts with no silicon, where fabless→foundry is N/A and the killed re-shape is noted — basis: the task asks for N/A with a reason and asks for re-shapes to be tied to existing kills.
