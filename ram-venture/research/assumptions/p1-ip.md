## Patent-term arithmetic
- [p1-ip] Expiry estimate = 20 years from the earliest non-provisional US filing or PCT date. For filings before 1995-06-08: the later of 17 years from grant or 20 years from filing. Patent-term adjustment, terminal disclaimers and maintenance-fee lapses are ignored, so real expiry can be earlier (lapse) or later (PTA, typically 0–3 years) — basis: 35 U.S.C. §154 [UNVERIFIED-training].
- [p1-ip] "Expired basics" means only the foundational concept claims are free. Improvement, integration and manufacturing patents filed 2006–2026 are assumed live and held by incumbents. This is why "expired basics" never scores as freedom-to-operate (FTO) — basis: standard patent practice; e.g., SEL's continuing CAAC-IGZO/DOSRAM filings in cross-ref sources.
- [p1-ip] The HfO₂ ferroelectric base filing is assumed to have ~2007–2008 priority (Qimonda Dresden discovery 2007, published 2011). Estimated expiry ~2027–2028. The current owner is unverified (Qimonda insolvency estate; FMC/NaMLab licensing status unknown) — basis: training knowledge.
- [p1-ip] The a-IGZO TFT base filings (JST/Hosono) are assumed to have 2003–2005 priority, so expiry ran ~2023–2025 — basis: Nomura et al., Nature 2004 timing [UNVERIFIED-training].
- [p1-ip] Toggle MRAM (Motorola, filed ~2001) is treated as expired ~2021. The STT concept (IBM/Slonczewski, filed 1995) is treated as expired ~2015. The PMC/CBRAM base (ASU/Axon, filed 1996) is treated as expired ~2016 — basis: 20-year rule on remembered filing years.

## License-term and timing ranges
- [p1-ip] University startup license ranges used in §6.4: option $1k–$10k; issue fee $0–$50k, often deferred; past patent costs $10k–$150k, often deferred; equity 2–10% (≈5% typical); royalty 1–5% of net sales (1–3% typical for semiconductor components); sublicense share 10–50%; annual minimums $5k–$50k — basis: general AUTM-community practice from training. No 2025–26 survey was retrieved [UNVERIFIED-training].
- [p1-ip] Deal timing used: option 2–8 weeks; exclusive startup license 2–6 months; national-lab license 3–9 months; CRADA 3–12 months — basis: training-knowledge ranges plus the extra federal approval steps at DOE labs [UNVERIFIED-training / ESTIMATE].
- [p1-ip] FTO search plus opinion, one narrow product: $10k–$30k for a scoped search and memo; $30k–$100k for a full opinion — basis: law-firm norms from training [UNVERIFIED-training].
- [p1-ip] Defense cost of one NPE patent suit through trial: ~$0.6M–$4M depending on stakes. Typical early settlements for small defendants: $50k–$500k — basis: recalled AIPLA economic-survey bands [UNVERIFIED-training].
- [p1-ip] T0 IP budget computed as option $5k + scoped FTO $15k + provisional filings on own improvements $5k = $25k, i.e. ~half of the founder's <$50k — basis: midpoints of the ranges above [ESTIMATE].
- [p1-ip] USPTO large-entity maintenance fees after the Jan-2025 fee rule: ~$2,150 / $4,040 / $8,280 at 3.5 / 7.5 / 11.5 years. Small entity pays 40% of these and micro entity 20% — basis: training knowledge [UNVERIFIED-training].

## Judgment calls on licensability
- [p1-ip] "Closed" means no licensing program exists and no license to a non-IDM startup has been seen (Samsung, SK hynix, Micron, Kioxia, TSMC, Intel XPoint, ST ePCM, GF CBRAM). "Likely" means a licensing business or a tech-transfer office exists and has licensed to small firms before. "Open" means a published license (BSD/Apache) or a PDK-integrated commercial IP — basis: author definition.
- [p1-ip] NEO Semiconductor and FMC are scored "closed to this founder". Both license only to fab-owning memory makers or foundries — basis: cross-ref sources (A1: NEO licenses to memory makers; eeNews 2017: FMC "ARM of memories" model aimed at foundries/IDMs).
- [p1-ip] Everspin is scored "likely, but at defense-prime scale". The $40M Amentum process-transfer subcontract (Apr-2026) proves Everspin will license process tech. It does not show terms a <$2M company could meet — basis: cross-ref electronics360/MRAM-Info.
- [p1-ip] Weebit ReRAM is scored "open" for a fabless U.S. startup through the SkyWater S130 PDK. Weebit's royalty/license terms for small fabless users are unknown and flagged — basis: cross-ref Weebit/SkyWater releases 2023.
- [p1-ip] Sandia HZO FeRAM is scored "likely" via license plus CRADA. Its availability on Sandia's licensing portal is unverified — basis: cross-ref Sandia news 2026-05-13 plus DOE tech-transfer norms.
- [p1-ip] The status of Nantero, Spin Memory's IP, Antaios, T-RAM's IP, Panasonic ReRAM and Kepler Computing is treated as UNKNOWN. None is used as a positive lead without verification — basis: no access.
