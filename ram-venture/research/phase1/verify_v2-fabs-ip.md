## Notes that bear on venture decisions

1. **SkyWater + Weebit is still the only open, qualified U.S. BEOL eNVM.**
   - IonQ's ownership (closed 2026-07-31) adds a strategic-owner risk: a quantum company now sets priorities at the "best open host" fab.
   - SkyWater no longer files public financials.
   - The neutrality concern in the press is about quantum rivals, not memory customers. I rate it **low-moderate** for a memory startup [ESTIMATE].
2. **The cheap prototype route for ReRAM on S130 (Efabless chipIgnite) died with Efabless.**
   - Whether ChipFoundry offers the Weebit block is unverified.
   - Until confirmed, ReRAM prototyping probably means a direct SkyWater MPW/engineering run plus a Weebit evaluation licence. That moves the cost from ~$15k toward a T1 budget [ESTIMATE].
3. **Weebit fees are material for a founder with <$50k.**
   - Analyst-estimated upfront fees run from ~US$0.3M (DB HiTek, a foundry) to US$1.8–2.6M (onsemi, an IDM), plus NRE, plus royalties.
   - A fabless startup's fee is unknown and may be lower or covered through the foundry, but it is **not** T0 [ESTIMATE].
4. **U.S.-made STT-MRAM slips to ~late 2028**, not 2H-2027. Gresham stays closed to other MTJ makers until ≥2038 (10-year term + 2 years).
5. **Sandia should be downgraded from "best single lead" to "unconfirmed lead".** No public licensing listing exists, and the work is internal LDRD.
6. **Cheapest open silicon is now GF180 via wafer.space** (US$7k for 1,000 dies). sky130 via ChipFoundry runs ~US$15k per 100 chips.
