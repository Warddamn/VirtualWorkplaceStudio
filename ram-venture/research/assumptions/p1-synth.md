The contract-based line is stricter and matches ASSUMPTIONS.md A-05, which is defined on contract $/GB. p1-market's $11.4/GB is kept as the upper bound. — basis: A-05; spot is about 1.7× contract in Aug-2026 (model §6).

## Judgment calls in the synthesis
- [p1-synth] **Premise verdict: intact and stronger on price and margin, but the project should re-aim its search, not its mission.** The "make cheaper RAM in the U.S." reading is closed (no U.S. DRAM foundry, no display fab, no die supply, no U.S.-origin premium, CHIPS/§48D closed). The open reading is "sell supply security, provenance and memory efficiency, plus niche non-volatile parts, and be cash-positive before the 2028 turn". — basis: §1 and §10 of 01_LANDSCAPE.md.
- [p1-synth] **The "Made in America" moat is re-scoped to "verified non-Chinese plus trusted/rad-hard".** TAA treats Korean, Taiwanese and Japanese memory as equal to U.S. memory; memory pays 0% under Section 232 Phase 1; only China-origin chips pay 50% (Section 301). — basis: funding_export.md §10; micron_us_makers.md §4.
- [p1-synth] **A byte of DRAM saved or supplied is worth ~$16/GB of contract value now and ~$6.4/GB in the glut**, which I use as the value anchor for memory-efficiency and recertification concepts in §10. It is a contract-level number; retail ($14–18/GB) and spot ($27/GB) are higher today. — basis: model §6.
