# Assumptions: v4-cost (Phase 1b cost verification, 2026-11-01)

- [v4-cost] The industry 1β 16Gb DDR5 die base is 36.5 mm² (0.438 Gb/mm²), the mean of Micron D1β DDR5 (35.83 mm², die seal) and Samsung D1b LPDDR5X (36.68 mm²). SK hynix 1b is assumed similar — basis: TechInsights figures seen in search snippets; ETN.fi says the Samsung D1b cell is "similar to SK hynix D1b".
- [v4-cost] The 1β high case keeps the old 41 mm² (0.39 Gb/mm²) to cover possibly larger Samsung/SK hynix DDR5 periphery. LPDDR5X and DDR5 are treated as equal density on the same node — basis: no SK hynix or Samsung 1b DDR5 die size found.
- [v4-cost] "Die seal" area is used, not full-die area (35.83 vs 36.00 mm²). The ~0.5% difference is ignored — basis: TechInsights reports both, and the model's scribe lane covers the rest.
- [v4-cost] 1γ/1c 16Gb base density is 0.55 Gb/mm² (29.1 mm²). Micron's ">30% bits per wafer over 1β" implies ≥0.58. The base is discounted for Samsung's reportedly larger 1c die — basis: Micron 1-gamma brief via snippet; Samsung enlargement is p1-cost prior knowledge [UNVERIFIED].
- [v4-cost] Micron's "bits per wafer" claim is read as a gross-die ratio, ignoring yield differences — basis: standard usage in Micron node announcements.
- [v4-cost] 24Gb and 32Gb dies scale linearly with 16Gb density, with no array-efficiency gain for larger dies — basis: no 24/32Gb die area found (Samsung 32Gb ISSCC 2024 paper 13.2 gives no area in snippets).
- [v4-cost] The Samsung 16Gb DDR5 die of 48.90 mm² (XenoSpectrum/3DNews; node not stated) is used only as the low end of the 1α range — basis: its density (0.327 Gb/mm²) fits 1α, not 1β.
- [v4-cost] The 4Q26 64GB RDIMM contract anchor is $1,650 (low $1,500, high $1,930) — basis: Counterpoint's Apr-2026 3Q26 forecast (~$1,500) × TrendForce 4Q26 conventional DRAM +10–15% gives $1,650–1,725; Citi 4Q26F is $1,586. Low = 3Q26 flat (buyers sought flat 2H26). High = Counterpoint 1Q26 actual $927 × TrendForce 2Q (+60.5%), 3Q (+15.5%) and 4Q (+12.5%) midpoints.
- [v4-cost] Blended conventional-DRAM QoQ forecasts apply to server RDIMM contract prices — basis: same convention as p1-model. Server DRAM was the tightest segment in 2026, so this is if anything conservative.
- [v4-cost] Derived DDR5 16Gb 4Q26 contract price = (RDIMM anchor − $20.6 non-DRAM BOM and assembly) / 40 dies — basis: p1-cost method (cost_stack.md §7) with the model's non-DRAM BOM.
- [v4-cost] The claimed October TrendForce revision of 4Q26 DRAM contract prices to "~+20%" is unconfirmed. It is used only as the high end of `contract_dram_qoq_4q26f` — basis: the only source is an AI-generated StockHub summary (2026-10-20); no TrendForce release was found.
- [v4-cost] EUV scanner price is ~$290M, from SK hynix's KRW 11.95T (~$8.8B) order for "30+" tools. The "+" is ignored, and service or High-NA mix in the package is not separated — basis: S&P Global 2026-03 via snippet. If the order holds more than 30 tools, the unit price is lower.
- [v4-cost] The 2026 28nm 300mm foundry base is $2,800, a blend of TSMC ~$3.0k (Silicon Analysts estimate) and UMC/SMIC/Hua Hong ~$2.0–2.5k after the 2026 hikes of up to +10% — basis: no non-TSMC 28nm absolute price found.
- [v4-cost] The 200mm 180nm absolute price stays at $700; only the high end rises to $1,150 for the confirmed 2026 hikes of +5–20% — basis: no public 180nm $/wafer found. The hikes are relative and diverge by foundry (TrendForce Mar-2026).
- [v4-cost] HBM3E 2026 price range is $8.33–12.8/GB. Fubon's $17–18/GB is treated as buyer-side system cost and kept out of the range — basis: DigiTimes via Capital & Compute; Silicon Analysts; TrendForce News (Fubon).
- [v4-cost] The 72Mb QDR-II+ broker price ($12.36 @1,088 to $33.70 @1) stands in for discrete SRAM $/MB, with 72Mb = 9 MB — basis: no 288Mb QDR price found; broker prices are volatile and quote-based.
- [v4-cost] Wafer-cost inputs ($3.4k/$3.8k/$4.3k) are left unchanged, although the model's 4Q26 GM (93%) now sits 6–7 pp above reported Micron/Nanya GMs — basis: no public DRAM wafer-cost estimate found, so changing them would trade one guess for another. The gap is flagged instead.
- [v4-cost] A new confidence tag, REPORTED-P (primary document seen only through a search snippet), is added to cost_inputs.json. `models/dram_cost_stack.py` treats it like REPORTED when it displays a source — basis: task instruction for Phase 1b.
