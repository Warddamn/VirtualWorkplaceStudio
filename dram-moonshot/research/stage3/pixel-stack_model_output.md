## 9. GO / NO-GO (revised after red team)

Headline: Cs 0.60 fF fits a 88 nm lateral capacitor (EOT 0.60 nm); per-tier footprint 0.0135 um2; 0.0320 Gb/mm2 per tier (array eff 0.50); 1-beta break-even at 14.0 tiers; 64 tiers = 2.05 Gb/mm2 (4.6x 1-beta) at 0.96x 1-beta $/bit ($1.42/GB, wafer $13,500, stack yield 0.807); signal 64 mV; modelled readout residual 15.8 mV (24.5 mV without decision feedback) against 39.6 mV allowed; tail retention 0.52 s at 85 C.

**GO/NO-GO line (decisive experiment: planar IGZO pixel-amp array, 85 C, revised).** Measure the post-CDS readout residual = joint per-sense 2e-07 quantile of (Gaussian read noise + time-domain RTN, after the BIST screen) + worst-pattern coupling after two-pass DFB, on 3-3.5 fF floating nodes read by oxide SFs of gate area <= 0.02 um2, captured by an on-coupon dual sample-and-hold with a 15 ns gap (65k nodes x >= 1e4 reads). **GO (0.45 fF) if <= 20 mV** (keeps ~9 mV of the 29 mV allowed for 3D-only effects). PLAN (0.60 fF, 88 nm capacitor) at 20-40 mV. Fallback (<= 0.84 fF) at 40-55 mV. **NO-GO for the oxide amp tier above 55 mV**: at >= 0.84 fF the 0.0162 um2 cell has no edge over an incumbent long vertical BL read by an ideal bonded OCSA (0.0147-0.0177 um2, sec.10). RTN sub-gate: <= 1% of SFs carry a fast-trap step > 8 mV (remap budget); large-area alpha_H on the same coupon <= 6e-3, else the result is 'process-limited' and the fab-grade split decides. Retention gate, same run, after a 4x cumulative group thermal/H dose, measured by write-wait-CDS-read: 1e-4-quantile node leakage <= 45 aA (1 s at 0.60 fF) is GO; <= 90 aA (0.5 s) is PLAN; > 225 aA (< 0.2 s, refresh busy ~ DDR5) kills the refresh claim. Revival path for a read NO-GO: the Smart-Cut c-Si sense layer of I-11545 (N-48).
