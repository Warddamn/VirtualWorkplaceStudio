# Assumptions — p1-market (Phase 1 market, prices, margins, cycle)

- [p1-market] All facts in `phase1/market.md` come from web-search result snippets. WebFetch could not resolve trendforce.com, technews.tw or koreatimes.co.kr from this session, and the shared web-search budget ran out after about 46 searches. So no primary page was opened, and no fact gets a tag higher than [REPORTED]. A snippet of a primary release (TrendForce press center, SEC EDGAR, company newsroom) is marked "primary-source snippet". — basis: session tool limits (WebFetch DNS failure; "web search budget is used up" message).
- [p1-market] Quarterly TrendForce ranges are reduced to their midpoint when compounded (for example, +93–98% is used as +95.5%). — basis: standard practice; TrendForce publishes ranges only.
- [p1-market] Unless I found a later "actual" figure, the TrendForce *forecast* for 2Q26, 3Q26 and 4Q26 stands in for the realized change. Only 4Q25 (+45–50%) and 1Q26 (+93–98%) have confirmed retrospective figures. — basis: TrendForce press releases of 2026-02-26 and 2026-06-01. The 2Q26 forecast is consistent with 2Q26 industry revenue +59.5% QoQ.
- [p1-market] "Late 2025" in mission §1 bullet 1 means the 4Q25 contract level, and "through 2026" means the 4Q26 forecast level. I also show a 3Q25 base. — basis: plain reading of the premise.
- [p1-market] Chip-to-$/GB conversion: 8Gb = 1 GB, 16Gb = 2 GB, 1 Gb = 0.125 GB, so $/Gb × 8 = $/GB. Module $/GB = module price / module GB. — basis: definition.
- [p1-market] DRAMeXchange "session average" spot quotes are treated as indicative of small-lot spot trading, not of the contract prices that OEMs and hyperscalers pay. The 2026 spot quotes sit above retail $/GB (DDR5 spot ~$27–28/GB vs retail kits ~$13–18/GB), which marks spot as a thin, panic-priced market. — basis: comparison of BuySellRAM/DRAMeXchange 2026-08-31 snapshot vs. rampricesusa Sep-2026 and retail kit prices.
- [p1-market] The two DDR4 8Gb spot series ($16–21 on TrendForce's board in Jun–Aug 2026, vs. $42–44 per Mobius/BuySellRAM in Jul–Aug 2026) are assumed to be different DRAMeXchange line items, probably eTT/generic vs. branded 3200. Neither is treated as authoritative. — basis: the values cannot be reconciled otherwise, and the snippets do not label the variant.
- [p1-market] SK hynix margins for 3Q25, 4Q25 and 1Q26 (47%, 58%, 72%) come from a tracker quoted in a search summary. I could not identify which result page the tracker was on, so they are [REPORTED, attribution uncertain]. 2Q26 (76.3%) and 3Q26 (~79.7–80%) come from the company release via news. — basis: search snippets.
- [p1-market] Micron operating margin for FQ4-26 (~83%) and the FQ1-27 guide (~83%) are my own estimates from reported non-GAAP gross margin minus reported or guided opex. Micron's FQ3-26 operating margin of 81.2% is as quoted in Korean press. — basis: arithmetic in scratch calc; [ESTIMATE].
- [p1-market] Samsung DS operating margin (72.4%, 3Q26) = DS OP KRW106.5T / DS revenue KRW147.1T. DS includes foundry and System LSI, so the memory-only margin is higher. — basis: Samsung 3Q26 release (via snippet).
- [p1-market] Glut test: "DDR5 falls 60%" is taken as 60% below the late-2026 peak contract/spot level, not below the 2025 level. — basis: mission §2 rule 6 wording ("DDR5 falls 60%").
- [p1-market] HBM3E 2026 price ≈ $1.50/Gb and HBM4 2027 ask "mid-to-high $4/Gb" (I use $4.5–4.9/Gb) are reported *asking or negotiated* prices, not confirmed transaction prices. — basis: Korean press (Oct 2026) via snippets.
- [p1-market] Mobius Materials' "DRAM Market Report" is assumed to be free (possibly email-gated) content marketing for its marketplace, not a paid subscription. I found no price for it. — basis: it is hosted under /whitepapers/ on a marketplace site; no pricing found; [UNVERIFIED].
- [p1-market] The 2018–19 peak-to-trough is measured on TrendForce's 8GB PC module contract average ($68 in 3Q18 → $25.50 in Aug 2019, −62.5%), because I could not retrieve the 2018 chip-level contract peak. — basis: TrendForce 2018 release and DigiTimes 2019 (via snippets).
