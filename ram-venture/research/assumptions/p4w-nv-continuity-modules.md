# Assumptions: p4w-nv-continuity-modules (DD3 write-up)

The model inputs live in `research/assumptions/p4-nv-continuity-modules.md`. This file logs only the judgment calls made while writing `04_DEEPDIVES/nv-continuity-modules.md`.

- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] The PROFITABLE verdict is MARGINAL, not the research notes' "yes, as a small business". — basis: BASE never triggers a founder salary (model §[5]). The 36-month net of $79.1k is ~$34 per part-time hour at A-03's 15 h/week. 25% of m36 revenue is two LTSAs with no DLA demand evidence. The card's unit economics are strong (57–61% GM), so it is not a NO.
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] The kill-test deposit page states a target ship date of 2027-07 (model card launch m9), not the notes' 120 days. — basis: the 20–26-week MRAM lead time makes 120 days unreliable. A longer promised wait makes 15 deposits a stronger signal.
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] The kill test adds a 72-hour magnetic-field storage check (card next to a spindle motor or magnetic chuck). — basis: Toggle MRAM's tolerance of shop-floor magnetic fields was not researched [UNVERIFIED risk]. The check costs nothing.
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] The kill test pulls forward ~$5.3k of model spend from m2–5 and adds ~$1.4k of new spend, so the BASE cash low falls from $10.8k to ~$9.4k. — basis: arithmetic on model §[9] `one_time` against the notes §8 budget.
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] Supply-failure path: reprice to $299 and re-test demand for 14 days; <10 deposits at $299 means kill. — basis: the +30% price sensitivity (model §[6]) and the notes' reprice-or-kill rule.
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] Kill thresholds after launch are judgment calls anchored on model rows:
  - <20 cumulative cards by 2027-10-31, against 33 modeled
  - no breakeven by m17, the worst modeled case
  - realized blended card price <$175 for 3 months, against the price −30% case
  - m18 run-rate <$85k (conversion −30%) or <$60k (wind down)
  - returns >10% / >20%
  — basis: model §[4], §[6].
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] Roadmap gates take model values with ~10–25% slack, e.g. ≥30 DIPs by m6 against 34 modeled, and ≥25 cards by m12 against 33. — basis: model §[4].
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] Warranty is 2 years, matching MRO Electric's on its Fanuc SRAM modules. — basis: the MRO Electric listing; the model carries 3% of revenue for warranty and returns.
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] A Mitsubishi-format card may later test $299. — basis: the Q2MEM-2MBSN eBay listing at $403.81. Not in the model.
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] Outbound sales are capped at ~5 calls a week while the founder keeps the day job. — basis: A-03 (15 h/week).
- [p4w-nv-continuity-modules] Part-time fulfilment help (~$500/month) appears only on the S1 path (>100 orders/month). A DMSMS business-development advisor works on commission only, from m24. Neither is in BASE. — basis: judgment.
