**Expected-value method.** EV = S x P x D, each scored 1-5, as the founder's framing asks (step-change size x plausibility x what $1M can prove).
- **S, step size** on the best mission axis: 1 is <=1.3x, 2 is ~1.3-2x, 3 is ~2-4x or a direct 10x on a sub-metric, 4 is ~4-8x, 5 is >=8x.
- **P, plausibility**, the chief's probability that the key unknown passes: 1 is <10%, 2 is 10-25%, 3 is 25-50%, 4 is 50-75%, 5 is >75%.
- **D, $1M provability**, how much of the key unknown a <=$1M, 18-24-month program settles: 5 is a decisive binary for <=~$300k.

Ties go to the bigger step, because the founder's framing weights the size of the prize: AND-STACK ranks above RAW-BER, and CHARGE-RIVER above LEAN. Ranks 2-4 (60-64) are within scoring noise; treat them as a group.

## Portfolio thesis

One physics bet, one interface, three layers. The bet (C0 triage): reading a bit is a circuit problem already solved in principle, and write endurance is the wall, so every cell here writes through a semiconductor channel or junction, never through an insulator, and capacitor charge is traded for sensing circuit. The interface is a raw-BER-1e-4 contract with exported erasure flags. LAYER 1, keystones (ranks 1 and 4): MARGIN STACK and RAW-BER CONTRACT. They are the most plausible concepts in the set, one MPW and one FPGA error study prove them, and together they cut the charge every cell must hold ~10x and buy 1.88x of margin. LAYER 2, four EUV-free cells on one oxide process vocabulary (oxide ALD in holes, per-tier recess, H-lean mold), each with one go/no-go number: AND-STACK (select-plate coupling >=0.6, <1 LSB disturb), PIXEL-STACK (post-CDS residual <=15 mV), DIODE-TOWER (effective Schottky barrier >=1.0 eV) and CHARGE-RIVER (CTE >=0.999 at a 50 MHz stage clock). LAYER 3, routes that win on energy, capital and supply rather than $/bit: HOT-SIDE MEMORY (~12x interface energy, refresh-free at junction temperature), CELL-WAFER FOUNDRY (EUV-free cell wafers on U.S. logic periphery), LEAN ARRAY MODULE (helium/PFAS/EUV hedge), and the AI MEMORY DIET demand hedge. Seed ($1M, 18-24 months, ~$735k of experiments): DRAM Bender error study ~$80k; one shared 28/22 nm MPW margin/readout chip ~$275k, which reads every coupon; AND-STACK planar emulation ~$150k; one shared oxide coupon run ~$150k (Schottky diodes, H-lean vs PECVD-mold MLC drift, minimal CCD structures); H2 accommodation rig ~$80k. The remaining ~$265k covers two lean salaries and overhead. BEOL-on-logic tiers, carrier bonding, the full CCD matrix, 4-tier stacks and the bond daisy-chain go through user facilities, partners and grants as raise milestones. Honest ceiling: a >=3x bits-per-dollar step needs one cell to pass and reach >=32 tiers at >=99.8% repaired tier yield, and AND-STACK or PIXEL-STACK carries the 3-9x case. Every other concept wins on energy, capital, EUV or supply chain.

## The ten concepts

### 1. MARGIN STACK: segment-gain, double-sampled, ECC-budgeted sensing that cuts the cell capacitor ~10x on oxide-access DRAM  (`margin-stack`, EV 75)

**What it is.** MARGIN STACK trades capacitor charge for sensing circuit, on the oxide-access 4F2 cells that Samsung and Kioxia/Nanya are already building. It adds a same-step 3T local amplifier on every 16-32 cells, CDS that cancels the amplifier's offset and flags slow decisions as erasures, and a raw-BER-1e-4 ECC budget. The capacitor falls from 5.4 to ~0.54 fF (a 130 nm stub), and the AR-60 etch disappears. The stretch case is 0.13-0.25 fF. **Proof.** One 28/22 nm MPW (~$275k) plus the shared DRAM Bender study, with go at raw BER <=1e-4 at <=0.5 fF and >=80% of errors flagged. **Merge.** Merged from device STUB (primary physics) and system Margin Stack (framing, charge-transfer pre-sense and neutraliser). **Chief note.** Highest expected value: it needs no new physics, and its chip is the reader for three other concepts. The biggest risk is data-dependent bitline coupling that CDS cannot cancel.

*Key unknown:* The input-referred residual of a VCT-built local gain stage after CDS. It includes data-dependent BL-BL/WL coupling during charge sharing, which CDS cannot cancel. At <=~13 mV sigma-equivalent the cell can be <=0.25 fF; at ~40 mV it stays at 0.54 fF. Second: errors at raw BER 1e-4 must be iid and >=50% flaggable, because VRT or hammer bursts defeat a t=8 BCH. The RAW-BER CONTRACT study measures this.

### 2. HOT-SIDE MEMORY: refresh-free oxide gain-cell tiers in the backside-power carrier wafer, plus <=4 tiers in the logic BEOL  (`hot-side-memory`, EV 64)

**What it is.** Oxide gain cells hold >200 s at 95 C where silicon DRAM needs 16-32 ms refresh. HOT-SIDE MEMORY puts 8-16 oxide tiers in the carrier wafer that backside power already makes mandatory, plus at most 4 tiers in the logic BEOL. Defects are binned as capacity, not logic scrap. That gives 30-100 GB per 800 mm2 die at ~0.3 pJ/bit, ~12x below HBM's interface energy (~205 W per accelerator at 8 TB/s). It is a near tier for the hottest traffic, not an HBM replacement. **Proof.** 2-4 IGZO/ZTO tiers on finished 22FDX MPW logic die, measured for 1,000 h at 105 C (~$300k). The carrier bonding test is a raise milestone. **Merge.** Device and system versions were near-identical; the system inventor's carrier-first, <=4-BEOL-tier split is adopted. **Chief note.** The cleanest step on a mission axis. The risk is 2-bit drift after the backside-power thermal/hydrogen sequence (1 b halves capacity but keeps the energy win).

*Key unknown:* Oxide-cell level drift (PBTI plus hydrogen) at 105-125 C, measured after the full post-bond backside-power thermal and forming-gas sequence and while operating under ~1 W/mm2. It must be <=20-30 mV over >=1 s for 2 b/cell (<=~50 mV for 1 b), with <=0.1% logic-killing short defects per tier and per bond.

### 3. AND-STACK: single-etch 3D oxide gain-cell DRAM, AND-wired with a select plate per tier and 2 bits/cell (coaxial per-tier cell as fallback)  (`and-stack`, EV 60)

**What it is.** AND-STACK puts a capacitorless oxide 2T0C cell at every tier-hole crossing of a NAND-style stack made with one hole etch. A write plate and a select plate per tier make it AND-wired, so neither write nor read is a series string. At 2 b/cell, 32-64 tiers give 2.0-4.0 Gb/mm2 (4.5-8.9x 1-beta) and $0.12-0.45/GB at 64 tiers (3-12x bits/$, before tier yield). **Proof.** A $150k planar 2-tier emulation measures select-plate coupling, disturb and 2-b drift on H-lean vs PECVD molds within 9 months; the 4-tier vertical stack follows the raise. **Merge.** Device AND-STACK is primary. System Depreciated-Fab DRAM is merged as the per-tier-patterned coaxial fallback with its CMOS-under-array and depreciated-line path, and its 4F2 density claim is corrected. **Chief note.** This is the biggest prize in the portfolio. Ranks 2-4 are within scoring noise, and AND-STACK leads that group on step size. The risk is select margin on a 0.15-0.2 fF floating ring.

*Key unknown:* Select margin on a ~0.15-0.2 fF floating ring. It depends on the select-plate-to-ring coupling ratio (needs >=~0.6) and on neighbour-ring coupling (<=30 mV at ~40 nm tier pitch). Together these decide two things. First, whether 63 unselected cells, some storing '1', stay >=5 decades below the selected cell's ~uA read current. Second, whether 4 levels in <=1 V survive array-level PBTI/hydrogen drift (<=~60 mV over the refresh interval at 85 C).

### 4. RAW-BER CONTRACT: an SSD-style CXL controller, erasure-flag interface and typed pages that let weak bits ship  (`raw-ber-contract`, EV 60)

**What it is.** RAW-BER CONTRACT applies the NAND controller playbook to DRAM-class memory: an open raw-BER-1e-4 interface with SA erasure flags, row BCH plus page LDPC, tier-orthogonal codes, RFM-protected relaxed refresh and typed pages, in a 28 nm CXL controller. On commodity DRAM it cuts refresh energy 10-15x (1 s refresh). For new cells it is the 1.88x margin that MARGIN STACK, PIXEL-STACK, DIODE-TOWER and AND-STACK MLC all assume. **Proof.** An $80k DRAM Bender study of flaggable vs confident errors, then an FPGA CXL controller soak. **Merge.** Device RAW-BER CONTRACT and system ECC-Native Memory were the same concept; typed pages come from the system side, and the analog hammer counter from the device side. The JEDEC 'ECC-native' grade, the useful core of the cut Memory Bits Compact, is its standards route. **Chief note.** The keystone interface. The risk is that errors at 1e-4 are mostly correlated or confidently wrong.

*Key unknown:* The fraction of raw errors at ~1e-4 that are iid and flaggable rather than row-correlated, VRT or confidently wrong, at 2-8x tREFI and 85 C, and in oxide/3D cells. If >50% are correlated or confident, t=8 codewords burst, the erasure gain falls to ~15% parity, and the contract needs stronger codes.

### 5. PIXEL-STACK: image-sensor-style group-sensed 3D 1T1C (all-oxide source-follower + CDS amp tier every 16 tiers)  (`pixel-stack`, EV 48)

**What it is.** PIXEL-STACK reads 3D 1T1C DRAM the way an image sensor reads a pixel. Every 16 tiers, an all-oxide source-follower/select/reset amp tier treats the vertical bitline as a floating diffusion, and CDS cancels the oxide-TFT offset that previously forced Smart-Cut silicon layers. The in-tier capacitor shrinks from ~600 nm to 30-60 nm. 64 cell tiers give ~2.7-3.3 Gb/mm2 (6-7x 1-beta), and 3D break-even falls from ~26 to 9-11 tiers. **Proof.** The chief staged a $150k planar pixel-amp residual and leakage test, read by the MARGIN STACK chip, before the $500k 4-tier stack. **Merge.** Device concept, with the system Margin Stack's segmentation argument. **Chief note.** It is the 3D route incumbents already pursue, minus the bonded c-Si layer. The risk is oxide 1/f noise plus coupling above the 15 mV residual budget.

*Key unknown:* The residual after CDS of an oxide source-follower read of a 2-4 fF floating vertical bitline at 85 C: data-dependent neighbour-bitline coupling during charge sharing, plus 1/f noise. It must be <=15 mV at 3.72 sigma against the 55 mV signal. Second: node leakage <=68 aA (0.45 fF, 0.15 V, 1 s) in a 30-60 nm lateral capacitor after the full <=400 C stack.

### 6. DIODE-TOWER: one transistor and 16-32 linear-capacitor bits per pillar, steered by per-tier oxide Schottky diodes and read by a double-sampled gain FET  (`diode-tower`, EV 45)

**What it is.** DIODE-TOWER puts one access transistor under a pillar of 16-32 linear capacitor tiers. Per-tier oxide Schottky diodes steer charge only when a tier's plate pulses, and an in-hole gain FET with CDS reads the 76 mV pillar swing. It is the DRAM analogue of 1T-nC FeRAM without ferroelectric fatigue: 2-4x 1-beta at 16-32 tiers, 15 of 16 transistors removed. **Proof.** An $80-120k coupon run on the shared oxide wafer measures barrier height, reverse leakage at 85 C, forward write current and 1e12-pulse cycling within 3-6 months; the 4-tier stack is conditional. **Merge.** Device DIODE-TOWER and system Pillar DRAM were the same cell. The chief adopts the system inventor's stricter spec (image-force-lowered barrier, plus a forward-current requirement of ~14 nA at ~0.7-0.9 V) and keeps the device inventor's FE persistent variant. **Chief note.** The risk is that no oxide Schottky combines a >=1.0 eV effective barrier, write current and cycling stability.

*Key unknown:* Per-tier oxide Schottky reverse leakage at 85 C must be <=~20-27 aA per tier. That needs an effective barrier >=1.0 eV after image-force lowering, in an IGZO/RuOx, PtOx, PdO or Ru-free MoOx contact after <=400 C processing. The same diode must still pass ~14 nA forward for a <=10-20 ns write, and its barrier must not drift after 1e12 forward pulses. Oxide-Schottky cycling has never been measured.

### 7. CHARGE-RIVER: buried-channel oxide CCD strings scheduled like a drum for LLM weight and KV streaming  (`charge-river`, EV 40)

**What it is.** CHARGE-RIVER stores bits as charge packets in vertical oxide CCD strings, with no transistor or capacitor per bit. A buried heterochannel, fat zero, decision-feedback sensing and a regenerator every <=32 stages push CTE toward 0.999. A drum-style placement compiler makes it a DRAM-class streaming tier for LLM weights and KV: ~1.65 Gb/mm2 (~3.7x 1-beta, 4-8x for pure streaming). **Proof.** Planar CCD CTE vs clock (4-200 MHz, 25-125 C) for surface vs buried channels: a minimal set on the shared coupon run, the full matrix ~$300k. A trace simulator checks schedulability. **Merge.** Device CHARGE-RIVER and system Circulating Tensor Memory were the same idea. The chief adopts the device inventor's 32-stage lap and a 50 MHz *stage* clock as the go spec. **Chief note.** It is the boldest cell and the cheapest to kill. The risk is oxide trap kinetics holding CTE near 0.98-0.99.

*Key unknown:* Charge-transfer efficiency per transfer in a buried IGO/IGZO (or ITZO/ZTO) heterochannel at >=150 M transfers/s (a 50 MHz stage clock) and 85 C. Go is >=0.999 with decision feedback, or >=0.9999 without. Trap kinetics, not mobility, set it: transport over a 30 nm gate takes ~15 ps, yet imec's planar surface channel shows only >0.98 at 4-6 MHz. Second: the schedulable share of LLM serving traffic must be >=92-94%.

### 8. LEAN ARRAY MODULE: helium-free (H2 backside cooling), PFAS-light, EUV-free process modules for periodic memory arrays  (`lean-array-module`, EV 40)

**What it is.** LEAN ARRAY MODULE is a set of process modules that keep an array line running through the 2026 chokepoints. It replaces helium backside cooling with low-pressure H2 (free-molecular conductance ~2.1x helium per unit accommodation). It removes the AR-60 fluorocarbon capacitor etch with stub or capacitorless cells, a carbon mold or AAO. It patterns purely periodic layers by IL/NIL, on Ga-free channels. It is not a $/bit revolution; it removes single-source exposure. **Proof.** An $80k gas-conductance rig measures H2 accommodation on coated coupons, then a partner-hosted 300 mm chamber swap. **Merge.** Device LEAN ARRAY MODULE plus the chokepoint-free recipe the system inventor had folded into Merchant Cell Wafer. **Chief note.** It attacks the one resource A2 finds binding. The risk is low accommodation or H2 plasma leak-in.

*Key unknown:* The thermal accommodation coefficient of H2 on real, film-coated wafer backsides (unmeasured, ~0.1-0.3). It decides whether H2 at 5-30 Torr matches helium wafer temperature under 2-5 kW bias without arcing or etch drift. Second: the plasma effect of ~0.5-2 sccm of H2 leaking into fluorocarbon etch chemistry (H scavenges F and shifts polymerisation and selectivity), and the 4% LFL on exhaust.

### 9. CELL-WAFER FOUNDRY: litho-light periodic oxide cell wafers bonded to U.S. foundry periphery under an open bitline-group bond spec  (`cell-wafer-foundry`, EV 36)

**What it is.** CELL-WAFER FOUNDRY splits DRAM at the bitline-group bond. Litho-light periodic oxide cell wafers from NAND-class tools (no EUV) are hybrid-bonded at 1-2 um pitch to periphery from U.S. logic foundries, under an open electrical spec. That removes 5-7 EUV layers (19-27% of 1c wafer cost) and lifts array efficiency from ~0.51 to 0.7-0.85. **Proof.** A spec draft plus daisy-chain/Kelvin bond vehicles on warped oxide-stack coupons (~$350-450k, partner-hosted); the bonded sense test comes after the raise. **Merge.** Device CELL-WAFER FOUNDRY and system Merchant Cell Wafer were the same concept. On bond pitch, the chief sides with the device inventor for vertical-bitline stacks and keeps the system inventor's mux-plus-MARGIN-STACK route for planar arrays. The chokepoint recipe moved to LEAN ARRAY MODULE. **Chief note.** This is the scale path for any winning cell. Its risk is mostly the cells' risk.

*Key unknown:* Bond-link failure rate and overlay at 1-2 um pitch on multi-tier oxide stack wafers that carry ~5 um of stressed films (bow and warp). It needs <=~1e-5 failures per link and <=0.3 um overlay, without a >=250 C bond anneal shifting oxide Vt by >20 mV. Second, for the concept to pay at all, one portfolio cell must pass.

### 10. AI MEMORY DIET: delete DRAM's jobs in the AI racks that cause the shortage  (`ai-memory-diet`, EV 30)

**What it is.** AI MEMORY DIET cuts DRAM bits per served AI user with no new cell. It combines host-DRAM-lite GPU nodes, KV hibernation to flash prefetched on client typing/focus signals (2-20 s of lead vs <1 s from scheduler hints), a trained shared cross-layer KV latent and exact KV sharing across fine-tunes. Critic-corrected effect: ~22% of rack DRAM and ~2-3x fewer DRAM bits per chat user. **Proof.** ~$280k of trace logging, an 8-GPU vLLM+LMCache replay and a latent distillation. **Merge.** System concept only. The chief demoted in-PIM top-k to optional, because software top-k already takes most of that gain. **Chief note.** The surest near-term bit lever, kept as the portfolio's demand hedge. The risk is p99 TTFT on turns without client-signal lead time.

*Key unknown:* The fraction of turns where client typing or focus gives at least the restore time (~1-3 s for 10-30 GB of KV). Below ~80% coverage, TTFT p99 forces a DRAM KV tier back in, and p99 is a tail metric, so the uncovered turns need a fallback such as partial-prefix restore or recompute. Second, whether a retrofit shared latent holds RULER/LongBench beyond the ~2-7x where training-free methods stall.

## Where the inventors disagreed on physics, and whose side the chief takes

1. **Capacitor floor: retention vs reading (MARGIN STACK).** The system Margin Stack implies any 0.3-1 fF cell can be read like a 5 fF one. The device STUB shows that retention, not reading, sets the floor for silicon access: a 13 fA tail on 0.54 fF loses 0.15 V in ~6 ms. **I side with the device inventor.** The capacitor cut is only legal on oxide access (1-100 aA gives 0.8-81 s), so MARGIN STACK targets oxide 4F2 and 3D tiers. Silicon-access cells keep ~5 fF and get the AAO/carbon-mold route in LEAN ARRAY MODULE.
2. **Where the gain must live (MARGIN STACK, CELL-WAFER FOUNDRY).** The system inventor puts the neutraliser and CDS latch on a bonded logic wafer; the device inventor puts a 3T gain stage in the array. A 0.5-1 um bond cannot land a per-bitline circuit on 40-80 nm bitlines, so bonded periphery sees a muxed ~10-12 fF line. **I side with the device inventor that the decisive lever is local gain within <=16-32 cells** (in-array, or in-stack as in PIXEL-STACK). Charge-transfer pre-sense and the neutraliser stay as global-line add-ons. Both are measured on the same MPW, and the 'stable Cn/Cbl >=0.5' test decides whether the neutraliser earns its place.
3. **Achievable margin floor (MARGIN STACK).** Device: an 8 mV CDS residual x 3.72 plus 20 mV data-dependent coupling gives 50 mV, so Cs ~0.13-0.25 fF. System: a ~3 mV post-auto-zero offset doubled by the neutraliser gives 6 mV against a 26 mV budget, so 0.27 fF needs 64-cell segments. The two agree on ~0.25 fF as the stretch. **Planning case: 0.54 fF (ECC + local gain only).** Data-dependent coupling, which neither CDS nor auto-zero removes, is the measured go/no-go.
4. **3D gain-cell density per bit-tier (AND-STACK vs Depreciated-Fab DRAM).** The system inventor's coaxial 4F2 2T0C (0.0064 um2, 0.8 efficiency) reaches 1-beta at 4 bit-tiers and 3.9x at 2 b x 8. Yet the same card cites A3's generic vertical 2T0C, which needs >=15 bit-tiers (2 b x 8 = 1.07x). The device AND-STACK (0.0224 um2 per hole, 0.7 efficiency) needs 14. **I side with the device inventor and A3.** A two-transistor, four-terminal gain cell in 4F2 is undemonstrated, and per-tier ArF patterning makes the coaxial route's bits/$ lag (A3: ~1.56x at 3 b x 8). The single-etch AND-STACK is primary, and the coaxial cell is its fallback. Both inventors agree on vertical AND/NOR pillars instead of series strings.
5. **CCD lap length and clock definition (CHARGE-RIVER).** The device inventor regenerates every 32 stages (96 transfers) and quotes a 0.64 us lap at 50 MHz. The system inventor uses 64 stages (192 transfers) with a 50 MHz *phase* clock (3.84 us lap). The system card's '<0.2% misses' figure is the critic's number for imec's 6 MHz lap, not for 50 MHz. **I side with the device inventor:** <=96 transfers between regenerators (0.999^96 = 91% vs 0.999^192 = 82.5%), and a go spec of CTE >=0.999 at a 50 MHz *stage* clock (150 M transfers/s). That needs ~92-94% schedulable accesses for a ~40 ns average, against ~98-99% at the slower definition.
6. **Schottky selection spec (DIODE-TOWER).** Both inventors put the bar at a 1.0 eV barrier, and their leakage arithmetic agrees: ~1-2 aA at 1.0 eV vs ~22-56 aA at 0.9 eV at 85 C, against a 20-27 aA budget. **I side with the system inventor's stricter spec.** The barrier is the effective, image-force-lowered one, so the zero-bias barrier must be ~1.05-1.1 eV. The diode must also pass ~14 nA forward (~0.7 V at ideality 1, ~0.9 V at n = 1.3) for a 10 ns write, which a high barrier fights. The coupon test measures all three.
7. **Yield coupling on logic (HOT-SIDE MEMORY).** The device inventor leads with monolithic BEOL tiers, arguing tiers fail open and only shorts kill (>=99.9% per tier keeps 98.4% of logic at 16 tiers). The system inventor leads with the carrier and caps BEOL at <=4 tiers. **I side with the system inventor.** 'Only shorts kill' is unmeasured, and hydrogen and 400 C anneals shift logic Vt regardless of memory function. The separately tested carrier, plus fail-soft binning, is the safer default, with BEOL tiers as an add-on.
8. **Bond pitch (CELL-WAFER FOUNDRY).** Device: 1-2 um is enough, because vertical-bitline stacks need one bond per bitline group. System: 0.5-1 um with an 8-32:1 array-side mux and 0.6 fF cells via MARGIN STACK. **Both are right for different arrays.** The device inventor's case holds for vertical-bitline 3D cell wafers, which is why the merchant split's first product should be a 3D cell wafer. The system inventor's case is the route for planar 4F2 arrays. Both require repair or known-good die, because W2W without it compounds 0.9 x 0.9 = 0.81.
9. **Panel overlay (cut concept).** The device inventor requires <=75 nm tier-to-tier overlay per field. The system inventor removes inter-tier overlay by making every tier a self-contained memory joined by coarse vias. **I side with the system inventor on architecture.** The overlay requirement is real only within a tier, where a single multilevel stamp aligns by construction. But this costs array efficiency (25-35%), so $/GB lands at parity ($0.6-2.4/GB), not the device inventor's $0.79. That result, plus unshown defect density, is why it was cut.

## Strong concepts that did not make the cut, and why

Nothing below is killed on physics. Each is out-ranked under this lens or absorbed, and each carries the condition that would promote it.

- **Self-Aligned Panel Memory / MEMORY BY THE SQUARE METRE (device + system).** S3 x P2 x D2 = 12. Both inventors agree it is dead above F ~0.3 um. At 0.25 um, tier-independent architecture gives parity $/GB at best, because n-only periphery costs array efficiency. Single-stamp multilevel imprint is proven only near ~1 um, and its killer-defect density at 0.25 um is unknown. No U.S. Gen-8.5+ line exists, and the demo ($450-800k) would eat most of the seed. **Revival condition:** <=0.1 killer defects/cm2 per tier at <=0.3 um multilevel imprint on >=200 mm glass, at <=$50/m2 per tier-pass.
- **Second-Life Bits (system).** S2 x P3 x D4 = 24. A credible supply route (~2-4 EB/yr, 8-15% of world output [ESTIMATE]; the critic puts the phone stream at ~3%), but it is not a new way to make memory, and its trust and controller pieces ride on RAW-BER CONTRACT. Run it as a partner or spin-out program on the same FPGA platform. **Promote if:** recertified all-in cost <=$3/GB at >=70% pass/reball yield, with the retention-PUF fingerprint stable over 3 months of VRT.
- **Memory Bits Compact (system, policy).** S1 x P2 x D3 = 6 on this lens: there is no physics to prove. Its useful technical core, a JEDEC 'ECC-native' raw-BER grade, is folded into RAW-BER CONTRACT's scale path. The advance-market-commitment and effective-GB rating belong to the $10-50M raise and policy stage. **Promote if:** an entrant-fab IRR model shows a $1-5B tapered tranche clears the cost of capital through a 2012-style bust, and >=3 hyperscalers sign LOIs.
- **Depreciated-Fab DRAM (system).** Not cut, but absorbed into AND-STACK as the per-tier-patterned coaxial fallback cell. It brings CMOS-under-array, depreciated U.S. lines and the H-lean-mold MLC drift test with it. Its standalone density claim (3.9x at 2 b x 8 tiers) did not survive the A3 cross-check.
- **Racetrack in the capacitor hole (triage N-82 / I-11581).** Triage top-10, ALIVE-SPECULATIVE: 10-25 bits per hole, no refresh. Deterministic domain-wall shifting at ~20 nm diameter with stochastic pinning is unshown, and it needs a magnetics line outside the portfolio's oxide vocabulary. Kept as CHARGE-RIVER's alternate device under the same placement controller. **Promote if:** a shift-error rate <=1e-9 per step in <=30 nm electroplated wires.
- **Click-RAM (triage N-81).** A charge-free bistable nanoribbon gate (45-80 kT barrier, aJ writes, hammer-immune). The risks are quantified mechanics, but there are four of them, all unshown: q^4 strain sensitivity, stiction, ring-down and fatigue. It is a MEMS/2D-materials program with no shared instrument in this portfolio. **Promote if:** a released-ribbon array shows >=1e12 snap cycles without stiction and <=10% switching-voltage spread.
- **AFE-reservoir stub (triage N-01) and IGZO gated-diode strip (N-03).** N-01 gives 3-6x charge per area, but its full-transition endurance (~5e9 best seen) is 10-1e4x short. It is kept as DIODE-TOWER's optional dielectric upgrade. N-03 is a capacitor-module-free 3D tier with a thin 60-80 mV signal. It overlaps PIXEL-STACK's group sensing and AND-STACK's capacitorless tier, and can be tested as a backup cell read by the MARGIN STACK chip, with no new instrument.
- **In-DRAM KV top-k (I-09070) and retired-GPU KV servers (I-00766).** System ideas with real gains, but critics showed software top-k already takes 8-16x of the traffic cut, and retired GPUs still rent in a compute shortage. They stay as AI MEMORY DIET options, not concepts.
