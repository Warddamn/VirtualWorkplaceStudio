## 10. GO / NO-GO for the decisive experiment (revised after red team)

Measured on >=64 kb oxide 2T0C arrays after the FULL sequence (<=400 C tiers, 350-400 C bond anneal, thinning, 400 C backside-metal + forming-gas emulation), at 105 C and 125 C:

- **GO (2 b/cell):** cell-to-cell level drift sigma over a 1 s hold at 105 C <= 20 mV (Gaussian raw BER <= 5e-05 in a measured >= 600 mV window; <= 2 mV if the window is only 400 mV), AND a 2-b refresh interval >= 0.2 s at 125 C with tail droop included (model at 15 mV: 0.25 s), AND logic ring-oscillator shift <= 1% with Vt shift <= 10 mV, AND logic-killing shorts <= 0.1% per tier and per bond. [revised after red team]
- **GO at 1 b (capacity halves, energy win intact; the PLANNING case after the red team):** drift sigma 20-75 mV (2-48 mV at a 400 mV window) AND 1e-4-tail retention >= 1 s at 125 C (tail leakage <= 22.5 aA).
- **NO-GO:** drift sigma > 75 mV (48 mV at a 400 mV window) at 1 b, OR 1e-4-tail retention < 1 s at 105 C, OR ring-oscillator shift > 1% that H-lean/In-poor process changes cannot recover. Revival: an H-blocking liner plus In-poor/IGO channel that restores <=25 mV, or a Ga-free ZTO/In2O3 channel that passes.
- **RED-TEAM additions to every GO line:** (a) RTN: >=100 repeated reads ~1 s apart plus fast reads at 1 us-1 ms spacing on >=1e5 cells at 105 C, 1e-4 RTN quantile <= 30 mV for 2 b (<= 100 mV for 1 b); (b) cross-temperature: write at T1, read at T2 (dT +/-30 K) with in-row references, residual sigma <= 10 mV after the 10 K rewrite rule (2 b), else 1 b; (c) write-FET Vt and SS on >=1e5 cells and a directly measured 1e-4 retention quantile: tail leakage <= 7.5 aA at 105 C (2 b, 1 s) and <= 22.5 aA at 125 C (1 b, 1 s), single vs series write gate, hold -0.5 vs -0.7 V; (d) a real 350-400 C fusion bond (D2O-labelled) on a tier coupon: top-tier dVt <= 10 mV and SIMS H/D profile, inside Gate A.

## Headline (revised after red team)

- Capacity: 20-78 GB per 800 mm2 die (8 x 1 b to 16 x 2 b carrier tiers), 39-157 GB per 2-die GPU vs 288 GB HBM4.
- Energy: 0.21 pJ/b at the port (0.37 delivered at 3 mm) vs 3.95 pJ/b HBM delivered: ~11x per bit delivered.
- Watts (capacity-indexed share): planning 16 x 1 b = 78 GB/GPU, 24% share, ~92 W per GPU; range 48 W (8 x 1 b) to 169 W (16 x 2 b).
- Refresh at 105 C: oxide ~103 s (1 b) vs Si 16 ms = 6,417x fewer refreshes.
- Key threshold: drift sigma <= 20 mV (2 b) / <= 75 mV (1 b) over 1 s at 105 C, after the post-bond thermal/hydrogen sequence, with RTN, cross-temperature and tail-leakage lines (s10).
- Model note: the 1 b retention row in s2 uses the reported (typical) cell; tail cells use tail_leak_mult.
