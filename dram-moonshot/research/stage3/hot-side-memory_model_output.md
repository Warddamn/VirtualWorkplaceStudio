## 10. GO / NO-GO for the decisive experiment

Measured on >=64 kb oxide 2T0C arrays after the FULL sequence (<=400 C tiers, 350-400 C bond anneal, thinning, 400 C backside-metal + forming-gas emulation), at 105 C and 125 C:

- **GO (2 b/cell):** cell-to-cell level drift sigma over a 1 s hold at 105 C <= 24 mV (raw BER <= 1e-4 in a measured >= 600 mV window; <= 13 mV if the window is only 400 mV), which is the same as a >= 1 s 2-b refresh interval with tail droop included, AND 1e-4-tail retention >= 1 s at 125 C, AND logic ring-oscillator shift <= 1% with Vt shift <= 10 mV, AND logic-killing shorts <= 0.1% per tier and per bond.
- **GO at 1 b (capacity halves, energy win intact):** drift sigma 24-80 mV (13-52 mV at a 400 mV window).
- **NO-GO:** drift sigma > 80 mV (52 mV at a 400 mV window) at 1 b, OR 1e-4-tail retention < 1 s at 105 C, OR ring-oscillator shift > 1% that H-lean/In-poor process changes cannot recover. Revival: an H-blocking liner plus In-poor/IGO channel that restores <=25 mV, or a Ga-free ZTO/In2O3 channel that passes.
