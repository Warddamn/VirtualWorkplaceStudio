Monolithic CMOS-under-array (oxide cells built directly on the periphery wafer, no bond) is the null hypothesis:
- 32 tiers: the bonded split costs $960/wafer more (second substrate + bond) -> the cell line must be >= $30.0/tier cheaper than the same tier in a logic foundry's BEOL (100% of a $30 tier)
- 64 tiers: the bonded split costs $977/wafer more (second substrate + bond) -> the cell line must be >= $15.3/tier cheaper than the same tier in a logic foundry's BEOL (51% of a $30 tier)
- 128 tiers: the bonded split costs $1012/wafer more (second substrate + bond) -> the cell line must be >= $7.9/tier cheaper than the same tier in a logic foundry's BEOL (26% of a $30 tier)
- W2W yield equals monolithic yield (no known-good selection); only D2W/KGD gives a yield advantage. The split's real case is tool
  specialisation (NAND-class high-AR etch/ALD tools logic fabs lack), parallel cycle time, >400 C or H-rich cell steps the periphery
  cannot see, and a merchant market -- not physics. This is the honest weak point of the concept and should be priced in.

- Reusable glass carrier (N-36) instead of a prime cell substrate saves at most ~$115/pair (1.5% of pair cost): a resource win, not a cost win.

- 1e7 links at 1.5 um occupy 22.5 mm2 of bonded area (one field); a 300 mm pair carries ~3e10 sites, so >=1e7 measured
  links per wafer is a probe-time problem, not an area problem.
- Design: chains of 1e3/1e4/1e5 links (to resolve 1e-7..1e-3) at 1.0/1.5/2.0 um on the same reticle, Kelvin 4-wire links for the
  resistance distribution, pad-to-pad combs next to signal and supply pads, electrical overlay verniers at die centre and corners,
  and IGZO/In2O3 FETs + 1T1C/2T0C cells in the coupon tiers for pre/post-bond Vt and retention.

## 9. GO / NO-GO

GO/NO-GO: GO if an 8-tier (~5 um stressed film) oxide coupon wafer, bonded W2W at <=1.5 um pitch to a periphery-emulator wafer,
shows: opens <=1e-5 per link (95% UCL over >=1e7 links) with no new opens after 500 thermal cycles; pad-to-pad bridges <=1e-7 per
adjacent pair (>=3e7 pairs); max overlay <=0.30 um on 100% of dies after backside stress compensation (bow <=~200 um);
>=95% void-free dies; and post-anneal (<=250 C) oxide Vt shift <=20 mV with 85 C retention change <=2x.
NO-GO for the 1T1C variant if opens >=1e-4 per link at 2.0 um, bridges >=1e-6, or overlay >0.5 um after best compensation (pitch
forced beyond ~2.5 um). Gain-cell variants hold to ~3.0 um. Either way the merchant split pays only if a portfolio cell passes.
Model margins: with 1% spares, repaired opens keep die yield >0.9 to a few 1e-3/link, so the 1e-5 open bar has >100x headroom for
random opens; it is kept as a proxy for bridge and reliability tails. Bridges onto global supplies, overlay on bowed wafers and
particle voids are the binding failure modes.
RED-TEAM ADDITIONS to GO (see s10): D2W is the primary bond mode for multi-tier wafers; W2W continues only if the anisotropic
(saddle) warp half-difference is <=~40 um after compensation (RT-PROC-01); bond-origin hydrogen traced by D-labelled bond surfaces
(ToF-SIMS) with dVt <=20 mV on top-tier cells AND selector/predecode TFTs after bond + 3 x 260 C reflow + 1,000 h at 125 C
(RT-DEV-01, RT-PROC-04); bootstrapped oxide sub-WL driver delivering VPP with <=50 mV level loss, <=10 ns edges and an active
<=-0.5 V clamp after 1,000 h at 125 C (RT-CIRC-01, RT-DEV-03); tier-stack 4-point-bend Gc >=5 J/m2 and FIB on every failed chain
(RT-DEV-04); opens/bridges counted only on CSAM-void-free fields, with particle density logged per wafer (RT-IND-04);
VPD-ICPMS <=1e10 at/cm2 In/Ga/Zn on bevel/backside before the bond line (RT-PROC-03).

Oxide sub-word-line driver (RT-CIRC-cell-wafer-foundry-01, RT-DEV-cell-wafer-foundry-03): the predecode is an analog switch in the WL path.
- Load per tier WL segment: 5,000 cells x 0.04 fF + 400 um wire = 260 fF; a 5 ns ramp to 3.0 V needs 156 uA,
  i.e. a pass TFT of W ~0.8 um at 200 uA/um (short-channel ALD In2O3, not display IGZO; display drivers are us-class because of pF
  panel loads and um-long channels).
- Level: a self-bootstrapped gate reaches ~3.0 + 0.7 x 3.0 = 5.1 V, so the pass TFT keeps 1.3 V of overdrive at VPP with an
  end-of-life Vt of 0.8 V. WL high = the silicon FX-line level; a 0.1 V Vt drift then changes R_on by ~8% (edge time), not the level.
  An active n-TFT clamp per WL ties unselected WLs to the silicon VNWL rail (<=-0.5 V) instead of letting them float.
- Area: 7.40e+06 WL segments x 0.45 um2 = 3.3 mm2 = 19% of the 17.2 mm2 array: it does NOT fit the
  8% edge budget; it needs a dedicated TFT driver plane over the array (costed in cell_common_adders, ~$60).
