This is my reading of the snippets; I did not read the rule text.

Independent die-level testing will therefore be bought for exceptions and risk cases, not as a blanket requirement — basis: Wiley and LexBlog snippets say no third-party audits are required and reliance on certifications is reasonable. Judgment: this shifts §5949 demand toward attestation and BOM-traceability software and away from lab screening.
