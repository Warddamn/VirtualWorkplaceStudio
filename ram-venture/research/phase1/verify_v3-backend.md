## Notes for decisions

1. **Seed S1 (provenance-certified recovered DDR4) passes its §6.5 price kill-check.** Realized used-RDIMM prices are $3.1–5.8/GB, above the $2/GB threshold. Re-running §6.6 gives a 17–23% contribution margin. Two cautions:
   - **Buy-side.** Iron Mountain's ALM nearly doubled on decommissioning volume *and pricing*, so the incumbents are capturing the repricing themselves. Buying lots at 60% of resale is optimistic.
   - **Glut.** The glut verdict stands: the business dies at about $1/GB.
2. **Used DDR5 RDIMMs are a new and larger per-unit opportunity.** They sell for $890–1,200 per 64GB, about 55–74% of new. Contribution per module is about $214–292 versus about $23 for DDR4. Supply is thin, though, because DDR5 fleets are only 2–3 years old.
3. **The price gap invites counterfeiting.** Used DDR4 RDIMMs sell at roughly 7–28% of DDR4 chip spot per GB. The 2026 re-soldered-DDR4-on-fake-DDR5 cases show that this gap is being arbitraged. That supports a provenance/test layer (S1/S2), but the documented 2026 counterfeits are consumer modules, not server RDIMMs.
4. **Small KGD lots are possible for legacy densities.** S4's entry capital may be lower than the 25-wafer-lot estimate. This applies only to 4Gb-class DDR4 and older, and the qualification-cycle kill still stands.
5. **Micron has locked up the long-lifecycle automotive buyer.** Its Strategic Customer Agreements cover Ford, GM and seven Tier-1s, and it is the only public DDR4/LP4 commitment. A newcomer's lifecycle opening is mid-size industrial/medical OEMs, and services rather than supply. This confirms the existing view.
6. **The Netlist hazard is unchanged or larger.** Samsung reportedly licensed Netlist in 2026, alongside SK hynix (2021). A new unlicensed DDR5 RDIMM maker would be an isolated target. Freedom-to-operate review remains mandatory.
7. **Amkor AZ is confirmed as not memory capacity, and it got bigger.** The reported investment is ~$12B, with NVIDIA's $1.5B and TSMC's 10-year deal attached. No HBM or DRAM packaging is confirmed.
